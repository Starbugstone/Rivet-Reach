# Cave lighting and crop light

User-requested fixes, 2026-09-13: planted crops must use nearby placed light, and covered caves must not receive outdoor illumination. These are working implementation decisions under that request. [Farming](FARMING.md) owns planting, stage times and harvests; [verification](verification/LIGHTING_RESULTS.md) records measured results and limits.

## Light access

Loaded chunks cache two independent four-bit channels per voxel: sky access and placed light. Open vertical columns receive sky level 15. Skylight spreads sideways and around cave entrances through nonopaque cells, losing one level per step. Opaque terrain blocks stop both channels. A sealed room has zero skylight; a shaft or side entrance admits light, which fades into the cave. Removing or replacing roofs and walls invalidates affected columns and neighbouring light boundaries.

Placed torches emit level 14, powered Workshop Lamps emit level 14 and lava emits level 15. Sources spread through open cells with the same one-level-per-step attenuation. Light follows an unobstructed voxel path, including around corners; it cannot pass through opaque walls. Held and placed torches retain warm shadowed point-light accents at intensity 27 and a fourteen-block range. These accents fade between 48 and 64 blocks and use eight stable slots; replacement fades out before a slot moves, then fades in over half a second. They no longer determine whether a surface receives placed light: the cached block channel provides continuous diffuse illumination across loaded terrain, through the terrain fog/view distance (304 blocks at default view distance 10). All loaded sources contribute without a camera-dependent source-count limit. Held/dropped items do not become persistent growth sources.

Crops, saplings and compost use a minimum light level of 9, taking the brighter of sky access and placed light. A torch therefore supports crops within five open-cell steps of its cell. Existing stage times, residency pauses, crop mutation budget and surface geometric-skylight growth rules remain unchanged. No irrigation, offline growth or new clock is introduced.

## Rendering

World surfaces sample both cached skylight and block light at their actual position. The already-fetched block channel supplies warm diffuse light on terrain, models, grass and water, with voxel wall occlusion and no extra field solve or additional field texture lookup. This persistent light remains visible even when every nearby point-light accent is disabled. Outdoor ambient illumination, environment reflections, direct sun/moon light and distance fog are reduced by cave sky access; local torch lighting and authored emission remain separate. A faint, cool-neutral ambient fill reveals unlit cave surfaces without sunlight. Its strength is independent of the day/night clock, with slight up/down shading for shape; it affects terrain, models, grass and water, with matching dim cave fog. The same minimum keeps outdoor terrain dimly visible on moonless nights. A selected held torch adds a soft warm fill fading over seven blocks around its flame, including direct-light shadows; switching away or emptying the stack removes it. This is a short-range artistic approximation of bounce light, not an additional simulated source or full indirect-light occlusion. The floor and held fill never enter crop, sapling, compost or spawn light queries. The existing URP directional shadows still determine directional occlusion near an opening. This is bounded voxel sky access, not a simulation of physical multiple-bounce global illumination.

Torch geometry is combined into four-material meshes per resident chunk, preserving the authored top/side attachment shape. Batches rebuild only when their attachment membership changes; origin shifts move the batch transform. Models extend through the terrain view distance and use the same fog. Presentation enumerates resident pages, never every historic torch attachment. Terrain retains greedy meshes. A shared packed GPU buffer and chunk hash table supply per-fragment light queries without additional terrain vertices, per-block objects or per-crop lights. Original world materials and first-person surfaces retain URP's BRDF and local-light handling. Offscreen item/portrait previews retain their own presentation lighting.

## Scheduling and lifetime

Terrain edits, changed lamp state and chunk residency enqueue work. A worker solves an immutable chunk snapshot using cached column heights and six neighbouring boundary snapshots. Changed boundaries enqueue their neighbours until the light field converges. Main-thread work publishes accepted results and uploads packed data; revisions reject superseded results. New edits can briefly precede their visual lighting update. Cached artificial/indirect light is withheld from growth while its page is invalidated.

Unchanged terrain performs no light solves. Moving the sun/moon changes presentation strength without rebuilding fields. Loaded chunks, including persistent chunk-loader tickets, own the cache; unloaded pages release their GPU slots. Uniform dark/sunlit fields share CPU arrays and are represented by a single value in the GPU chunk table. Nonuniform pages use one packed byte per voxel; expanding their buffer copies existing data on the GPU. Height lookups respect saved chunk generator versions and do not register or regenerate terrain. All light data is derived and rebuilt after loading; durable-save bytes, generator versions and world content definitions are unchanged.

## Electric Workshop Lamps

Powered lamps now use point intensity 30 (previously 2.5) and a twenty-block radius (previously 7). Brightness still scales with received watts and switches off without power; 20 W demand and optional signal control are unchanged. Their existing eight-active-point-light cap remains for nearby accents, with gradual slot transitions and the 48–64-block observer fade. Persistent cached diffuse lighting remains at distance regardless of this cap. Lamp models extend through the terrain view distance. This extends the existing unshadowed lamp presentation; placed gameplay emission stays level 14 in the shared cache.

## Shared spawn-light query

`VoxelWorld.TryGetSpawnLight(position, WorldClock.IsNight, out level)` supplies a resident, settled 0–15 simulation value: the brighter of placed block light and propagated skylight, with skylight contributing zero at night. Sealed caves already have zero skylight at any time. Species thresholds belong to [mob rules](MOBS.md); visual ambient fill, carried light, point-light intensity and camera pools do not change the result. Existing geometric-sky crop timing remains separate.

Before returning a value, the query checks resident light pages intersecting the 31-cell cube centred on the sample (±15 cells, at most eight chunks). Unsolved pages and changed/in-flight revisions reject, covering source changes before they reach a neighbouring border. Roof edits invalidate every resident page in their column. Nonresident neighbours follow the solver's zero-boundary policy; unrelated distant work never gates local queries. This adds bounded lookups when spawning is evaluated, not a world scan or another simulation tick loop.

## Shared surface-light sampling — 2026-09-22

Project PBR shaders enter through `RRFragmentPBR`, which reads the resident surface-light field once per fragment and shares the result with the directional and ambient-light adapters. This is fragment-local scratch, never cached across pixels or frames. Model fog keeps its separate positional offset and only reads the field where its blend weight is nonzero. Shadowing, BRDF response, emission, held-light fill and gameplay light queries are unchanged. [Optimization verification](verification/FACTORY_OPTIMIZATION_RESULTS.md) owns pixel comparisons and native measurements.
