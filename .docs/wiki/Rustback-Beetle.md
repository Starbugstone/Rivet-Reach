# Rustback beetle

[All mobs](Mobs.md) · [Dusk prowler](Dusk-Prowler.md) · [Floater](Floater.md)

The Rustback beetle is a territorial native creature with orange wing cases, six legs, antennae and mandibles. It can appear on supported outdoor terrain during either day or night.

![Rustback beetle in the game](images/mobs/rustback-beetle.png)

## Behaviour and combat

A nearby beetle warns you when you approach within five metres. Leave during its roughly 1.4-second warning to avoid provoking it. Remaining nearby or striking it starts an attack. It has **12 health**, and a successful bite deals **2 HP before armor**.

The bite has an approximately 0.8-second preparation. Step out of reach or obstruct the attack with solid terrain before it lands. Attack with the normal mining/attack button while aiming at the creature; selected weapon damage applies.

Beetles return toward home after being drawn too far away. They do not break terrain or attack machinery, and they currently drop **no item** when defeated.

## Wall climbing

![A beetle climbing an actual voxel wall](images/mobs/beetle-wall-climb.png)

Beetles can climb supported vertical walls and pull over a clear upper edge. Wall climbing works while wandering, chasing or returning home. Removing their supporting blocks releases their grip; unsupported gaps and solid overhangs block the route. They cannot crawl across ceilings.

A short wall is therefore not a reliable beetle barrier. Keep distance during the warning and watch for routes over nearby terrain.
