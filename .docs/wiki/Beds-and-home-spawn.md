# Beds and home spawn

Craft a [Bed](Item-bed.md) to make a home respawn point and sleep through the night. You need [Cloth](Item-cloth.md) from flax and [Planks](Item-planks.md).

## Craft and place

At a [Workbench](Item-workbench.md), place three Cloth in a row, with three Planks directly beneath them. This makes one Bed.

![Bed recipe in the in-game browser](images/beds/bed-recipe.png)

![Three Cloth above three Planks in the Workbench](images/beds/bed-crafting.png)

![The crafted Bed in the inventory](images/beds/bed-crafted.png)

Leave two empty, dry blocks above solid floors. Aim at the floor and place the bed; its pillow points away from you. Keep space beside it for getting up. Both halves must be clear of players and creatures.

![The placed timber bed with a blue quilt and linen pillow](images/beds/bed-placed.png)

Mining either half returns one Bed. Removing either floor also breaks the bed and drops one Bed. Pick it up normally. Crouch while placing against an existing bed to place a block instead of using it.

## Set your home

Aim at either half and **right-click/Use** or press **Interact**. During the day, this sets your home without advancing time. The game confirms the new spawn point.

![Daytime bed use confirms the home spawn](images/beds/bed-home-set.png)

After death, respawn in a clear spot beside your bed. If it was destroyed or every safe spot is blocked, you return to the normal world spawn and see a message explaining why. Leave floor space and headroom around the bed; nearby liquids and creatures can make a spot unsafe.

![Respawn beside the saved home bed](images/beds/bed-respawn.png)

Using a different bed moves your home. Breaking a bed and replacing it in the same place requires using the replacement again. Save through Escape before quitting to keep your bed and home assignment.

## Sleep until morning

Use a valid bed between **18:00 and 06:00** to set home and wake at **06:00**. Holding Use does not repeatedly skip days.

![The bed before sleeping at night](images/beds/bed-night.png)

![Morning after using the bed](images/beds/bed-morning.png)

Sleep advances the sky and calendar. It does not give crops, chickens or machines a night's worth of production, consume a night's fuel, refill hunger or heal you. Existing hostile creatures remain, so keep your bedroom safe.
