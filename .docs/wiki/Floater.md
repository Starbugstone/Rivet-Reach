# Floater

Floaters are hostile stone creatures: a hovering rocky sphere with a scowling face and heavy arms. They appear on supported outdoor terrain **at night**. Existing Floaters remain dangerous after sunrise.

![An actual Floater hovering above terrain in the September 13 review build](images/floater-hover.png)

## Fighting a Floater

A Floater spots a visible player within 14 metres, approaches at low height and draws back its arms before punching. Step back during that preparation or put solid terrain between you and the creature. Its punches deal 3 HP before armor and survival protections; it has 16 health. Attack with your normal mining/attack button while aiming within melee reach. Weapons use their usual damage.

The round body stays about 0.6 metres above the ground. It can follow one-block steps and route around walls, but cannot climb tall vertical walls or fly freely across deep gaps. It does not break blocks or attack machines. Creative mode keeps the player invincible.

## Floater Rock

Killing a Floater drops **one [Floater Rock](Item-floater-rock.md)**. Walk close to the fragment to collect it. It stacks to 64 and can be carried or stored in a chest. It is a crafting resource, not a fuel, food or placeable terrain block.

![Floater Rock dropped on the ground after a defeat](images/floater-rock-drop.png)

![The same stone fragment held in the player's hand](images/floater-rock-held.png)

Save checkpoints preserve living Floaters, their health and positions, and carried, stored or dropped rocks. Saving and loading during the death animation cannot award a second rock. Normal dropped-item hazards still apply, including destruction in lava.

These are actual in-game captures from the **2026-09-13 Floater review build**. Combat balance and artistic acceptance remain under review.

Use its rock with a Pump to craft a [Ranged Liquid Pump](Ranged-Liquid-Pump.md) for collecting finite liquid sources.
