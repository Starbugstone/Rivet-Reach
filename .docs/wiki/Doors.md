# Wooden doors

Craft **three Wooden Doors** from **six planks** in a workbench:

```text
Plank  Plank  Empty
Plank  Plank  Empty
Plank  Plank  Empty
```

Place a door above a solid floor with two empty cells of headroom. It uses one item. Aim at either half and **right-click** or press **E** (your bound Interact key) to open or close it. Holding right-click toggles only once. Crouch to place another block against a door.

Connect blue **Signal Conduit** or supported **Signal Wire** to the lower cell, then connect a lever or button. A change to ON opens the door; a change to OFF closes it. No electricity is needed. Manual use still works between signal changes. Removing a live cable sends OFF; attaching an already-OFF cable leaves the current position alone.

Closing waits if you or a creature occupies the doorway, then finishes when clear. Mining either half or removing its floor recovers one complete door. Saves preserve doors and their open/closed positions.

See [Blue Signal](Blue-Signal.md) for wiring. The industrial Workshop Hatch is a separate item.
