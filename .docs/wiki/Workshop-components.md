# Make your first workshop components

Start with the [Workbench](Item-workbench.md) and [Furnace](Item-furnace.md), then smelt iron and copper. At the Workbench, combine **one Workbench, four Iron Ingots and two Copper Ingots** to make a [Machinist’s Bench](Item-machinist-bench.md). Place it and press **E or right-click** to open its 4×4 grid.

## Cogs and Rivets

| At the Machinist’s Bench | Result |
| --- | --- |
| [Iron Ingot](Item-iron-ingot.md) ×1 in any cell | [Iron Cog](Item-cog.md) ×1 |
| [Iron Plate](Item-iron-plate.md) ×1 in any cell | [Rivets](Item-rivets.md) ×4 |

To make Iron Plates first, put **three Iron Ingots in a horizontal row** at the same bench. That produces **three Iron Plates**. These components need the Machinist’s Bench; the smaller Workbench cannot make them.

Find the component in the inventory’s item browser and open its recipe. **Fill grid** moves the required ingredients from your inventory; click the crafting result to make it. Shift-click the result to send crafted output directly to your inventory.

## Build machinery

[Cogs](Item-cog.md) are used by the Boiler Engine, Alternator, Crusher, Pump and other mechanisms. [Rivets](Item-rivets.md) and Plates make the [Machine Casing](Item-machine-casing.md), which supplies the structural component for many machines. [Copper Wire](Item-copper-wire.md) carries the electrical side of this progression.

Smelt **one Sand into one Glass** in a Furnace or Electric Furnace. [Glass](Item-glass.md) is an ingredient for lamps, indicators and [Reinforced Tank Glass](Item-tank-glass.md); ordinary Glass is not a standalone placeable window.

Existing saves keep every component and machine already owned. The revised recipes apply to new crafting; previously made Cogs and Rivets are not converted or refunded.
