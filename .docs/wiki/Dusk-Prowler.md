# Dusk prowler

[All mobs](Mobs.md) · [Rustback beetle](Rustback-Beetle.md) · [Floater](Floater.md)

The Dusk prowler is a dark blue native predator with swept ears, a mane and a long balancing tail. New prowlers appear on supported outdoor terrain **at night**. Existing prowlers remain dangerous after sunrise.

![Dusk prowler in the game](images/mobs/dusk-prowler.png)

## Behaviour and combat

Prowlers detect a visible player within roughly **16 metres**, pursue on the ground and bite. They have **18 health**; a successful bite deals **3 HP before armor**.

The bite has an approximately 0.65-second preparation. Back away or put solid terrain between you and the prowler before it lands. Attack using the normal mining/attack button while aiming within melee reach. Your selected weapon supplies its ordinary damage.

Prowlers can follow one-block ground steps and route around nearby obstacles. They cannot climb vertical walls like beetles, and they do not mine blocks or raid machines. They return toward home if drawn beyond their leash or after losing sight of you for long enough.

Prowlers currently drop **no item** on defeat. Their health, identity and intent survive Save/Load, subject to the [ambient population rules](Mobs.md#persistence).
