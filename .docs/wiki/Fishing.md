# Fishing

Turn flax String into a Fishing Rod and catch food from a proper pond, river or sea.

## Craft a rod

At a **Workbench**, combine **three Sticks and two String** to make one [Fishing Rod](Item-fishing-rod.md). The recipe uses a diagonal line of sticks with string down the right edge; the mirrored layout also works.

![Fishing rod workbench recipe](images/fishing/fishing-rod-recipe.png)

Place the ingredients in the Workbench grid, then take the rod from the result slot.

![Workbench loaded with sticks and string](images/fishing/fishing-crafting.png)

![Crafted fishing rod with its ingredients consumed](images/fishing/fishing-crafted.png)

The rod is reusable. You do not need bait, electricity or a separate fish farm machine.

## Find enough water

Aim at a patch containing **7×7 water source blocks, at least two source blocks deep**. That is 98 source blocks with an open surface. Flowing water, waterfalls, shallow puddles and lava do not qualify. A player-built pond of the same size works too.

Cast at the centre of the open patch from within **eight blocks**. A single water block on the ground is not enough.

## Cast, watch and reel

Hold the rod and press **Use**—right-click with the default controls—to cast. A float and line mark your fishing spot.

![Cast line and float on a qualifying pond](images/fishing/fishing-cast.png)

Wait **8–16 seconds**. When the float dips and **“Bite! Use now to reel in”** appears, press Use again within **three seconds**. Holding the button does not reel automatically.

![Bite cue and dipped float](images/fishing/fishing-bite.png)

A successful reel catches **one Raw Fish**. If your backpack is full, it drops nearby for pickup. Reeling too early or missing the bite gives no catch.

![Successful catch](images/fishing/fishing-catch.png)

Opening a menu, switching slots, walking more than twelve blocks away, obstructing the line or changing the pond cancels your cast. Saving retains your rod and fish, but does not retain an active cast or award catches while the game is closed.

## Cook your catch

[Raw Fish](Item-raw-fish.md) restores **2 food points**. A fuel-burning or electric Cooker turns one raw fish into [Cooked Fish](Item-cooked-fish.md), restoring **6 food points plus 2 saturation**. Raw Fish grants no saturation.

![Cooked fish recipe](images/fishing/fishing-cooked-recipe.png)

![Cooker with finished cooked fish](images/fishing/fishing-cooking.png)

For [Fish Stew](Item-fish-stew.md), combine **one raw or cooked fish and two vegetables** in either Cooker. Potatoes, carrots or one of each work. Stew restores **12 food points plus 4 saturation**. [Food and hunger](Food-and-hunger.md) explains the reserve and current meal cadence. Both fish recipes take ten seconds at full heat/power; use the ordinary cooker fuel, power and pipe setup described in [Farming and cooking](Farming-and-cooking.md).

![Fish stew ingredients and cooker recipe](images/fishing/fishing-meal-recipe.png)

Food does not spoil. Images show actual September 19, 2026 review-player gameplay; long-session fishing/food balance remains under review.
