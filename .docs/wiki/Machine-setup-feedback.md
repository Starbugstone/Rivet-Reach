# Machine setup feedback

Open a machine with Use or your bound Interact action. The left panel pairs its illustration with setup advice that follows its current state. It explains likely next steps while the existing input, output, power and signal readouts show the exact operating state.

## Electricity and production

An unconnected machine asks for a cable or fitted power connection. A connected cable with no supply asks you to connect a working generator or a discharging battery. Low power asks for more supply or less shared demand. A full output asks for room or an output pipe. If Blue Signal disables the machine, switch the signal on or remove that control.

![A Crusher distinguishes a connected cable from an actual power supply](images/alpha-guidance/crusher-connected-no-supply.png)

For item and fluid pipes, a selected [Wrench](Item-wrench.md) cycles the machine-facing end through blue **Input**, red **Output**, and **No connection**. A machine output needs a red end; its destination needs a blue end. Electricity and Blue Signal remain separate connections.

## Boiler, alternator and pumps

The Boiler guide identifies its rear fuel inlet, water requirement and right-side shaft. Place an aligned Alternator beside that shaft and connect its electrical cable to your consumers. The Alternator reports a missing, misaligned or inactive Boiler.

![The Boiler interface explains fuel, water and the Alternator shaft](images/alpha-guidance/boiler-shaft-fuel-water-guide.png)

The ordinary Pump draws source water directly below it and needs no electricity. A Ranged Liquid Pump searches for compatible liquid sources in its reach. Their feedback also calls out a full buffer or signal stop. A Drill needs a mineable deposit below it; extraction is finite.
