# Machine interfaces

Press **E** (Interact) or right-click a placed machine to open its controls. The left panel shows a large illustration and the name of the machine you opened. Your backpack stays in the middle, with that machine’s controls on the right and the item catalog beside them.

The picture uses the same artwork as the machine’s inventory item. It identifies the machine type; read the status and meters on the right for its current operation, power, stored energy or water. Tank and battery bank fittings show the particular part you opened.

## Processing and storage

![Crusher interface with machine illustration, backpack and input/output controls](images/machine-interface-crusher.png)

For a [Crusher](Item-crusher.md), put ingredients in **INPUT** and collect products from **OUTPUT**. The status explains what it needs to work. For powered setups, follow [Pipes](Pipes.md) and [Electricity and batteries](Electricity-and-batteries.md).

![Battery interface with its illustration and stored-energy controls](images/machine-interface-battery.png)

A [Battery Block](Item-battery-block.md) shows stored energy and its charge/output rates beside the mode controls. Its illustration stays the same as charge changes; the meter and numbers show the current amount.

## Starter stations

![Furnace interface with its illustration and separate ingredient, fuel and result slots](images/machine-interface-furnace.png)

[Furnaces](Item-furnace.md), [Chests](Item-chest.md), [Workbenches](Item-workbench.md) and the [Machinist’s Bench](Item-machinist-bench.md) use the same illustration panel. Their existing slots, crafting grids and controls work as before. Shift-click transfers items; the catalog still provides recipes and uses.

Close the station and open your personal inventory to see your character portrait again. Appearance and equipped armor remain available.
