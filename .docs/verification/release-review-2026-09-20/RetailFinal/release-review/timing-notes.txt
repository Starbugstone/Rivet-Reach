RetailFinal follow-up analysis, September 20, 2026
Source: retained frames.csv.gz and gpu-telemetry.csv.
Five-minute soak: 1,138 floor-breach rows; returned GPU timing median 27.083 ms across those rows, versus 12.646 ms for 17,844 within-floor rows. Returned timing samples are delayed and may repeat, so these grouped row statistics are supporting correlation, not exact per-frame attribution.
The twelve largest soak frame rows record zero machine/crate view creation. Maximum returned GPU timings in that region reach approximately 100 ms. Late-soak NVIDIA samples record thermal throttling and graphics clocks falling to 300–405 MHz. CPU render medians remain about 3.7–3.8 ms in both row groups.
This supports investigating rendering and thermal limits; it does not establish that every floor breach is GPU-caused or excuse the unmet 60/45 FPS gate.
